This document outlines the one-line claim, the detailed page-by-page content map, and the collection log of final assets needed for the portfolio build.

## 1. The One-Line Claim

## 2. Content Map

Every page and call-to-action (CTA) ladders directly to our primary objective: **getting safety leads to book a 15-minute technical demo.**

```text
               [ Landing / Hero Page ]
                          │
       ┌──────────────────┼──────────────────┐
       ▼                  ▼                  ▼
[ Telemetry Case ]  [ About & Research ]  [ Demo Scheduler ]
 (Primary Proof)     (CV / Credentials)    (Conversion Page)
       │                  │                  │
       └──────────────────┴──────────────────┘
                          │
                          ▼
             [ CTA: Book 15-Min Demo ]
```

### Page 1: Landing & Case Spotlight
1. **Hero Section:** One-line claim, monogram logo, and primary CTA button ("Book a 15-Minute Technical Demo").
2. **Elevator Pitch:** A 3-sentence summary of the value of safety telemetry and ML prioritization.
3. **Work Grid:** Visual cards for the 3 core cases:
   - *Case 1 (Lead):* FlyRank Search Telemetry & Refresh Opportunity Scorer.
   - *Case 2:* SecureDrive-CV: Real-Time Perception Shielding.
   - *Case 3:* Sovereign-Shield: Secure Enterprise AI Gateway.
4. **Closing CTA Block:** Secondary button linking to the scheduler page.

### Page 2: Case Study Deep Dive (FlyRank Search Scorer)
1. **Header:** Title and metadata (out-of-fold Precision@50).
2. **The Problem:** Detailed audit of editorial resource limits and the faked 98% random-split accuracy.
3. **What I Did & Decided:** Coding client-holdout validation and Random Forest training.
4. **Evidence & Visualization:** Model comparison table and Gini feature importances chart.
5. **The Playbook:** The programmatic triage actions and reason codes.
6. **Case CTA:** Button: "View Python Code on GitHub" + primary "Book a Demo" CTA.

### Page 3: About, Research & Bio
1. **Professional Bio:** Focused on academic credentials in AI Safety.
2. **Research Cases:** Technical beats of SecureDrive-CV (classification recovery from 12% to 98% under attack) and Sovereign-Shield.
3. **Publications:** Academic papers, thesis opponent rejoinders, and PDF links.
4. **Bio CTA:** "Download CV (PDF)" + primary "Schedule a Demo" button.

### Page 4: Scheduler & Contact
1. **scheduler Embed:** Active booking calendar widget (Calendly).
2. **Quick Form:** Contact options for safety directors who cannot use the calendar due to firewalls.
